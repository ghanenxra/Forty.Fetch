Built for Speed & Desktop Precision

Engineered without bloated web-wrappers. Native, lightweight, and blazingly responsive.

⚡

Up to 8K 60fps Resolution

Extract maximum video fidelity. Supports 4320p (8K), 2160p (4K), 1440p, 1080p 60fps with automatic FFmpeg video & audio stream merging.

🎵

320kbps MP3 Audio Extraction

Rip crystal-clear sound from music videos, concerts, DJ sets, and podcasts directly into high-bitrate MP3 format with embedded metadata.

🛡️

100% Ad-Free & Private

Zero telemetry, zero ads, and zero third-party tracking. All operations run strictly on your local machine using open source backends.

🚀

Multi-Fragment Acceleration

Downloads are split into up to 16 concurrent fragments to saturate your network bandwidth and deliver blazing download speeds.

🌓

Dark & Light Cyber Themes

Switch between the dark futuristic navy/cyan theme and a clean light slate theme with one click in settings. Zero restart required.

📺

Playlists, Channels & Shorts

Support for any YouTube URL structure. Easily fetch individual clips, full multi-video playlists, or vertical Shorts instantly.

🎬

Supported Quality Presets

FortyFetch selects the best available stream codecs (AV1, VP9, H.264) and highest quality audio (Opus / AAC) to produce seamless MP4 or MP3 output.
